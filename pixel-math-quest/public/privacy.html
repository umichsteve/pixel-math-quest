Pixel Math Quest Privacy Policy

Effective July 6, 2026

Pixel Math Quest is a math practice game for kids, built to be played entirely offline. Our privacy policy is simple because the app is simple: we do not collect anything.

Data we collect

None. Pixel Math Quest does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign ins, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third party services of any kind.

Game progress

Stage progress, stars, and best scores are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app, or using the Reset Progress button, removes it completely.

Internet access

The app does not require an internet connection to play and makes no network requests during gameplay.

Children's privacy

Pixel Math Quest is designed for children. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information from children is ever gathered, and there is nothing for a parent to request, review, or delete on our side. This approach is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version ships.
